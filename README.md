## 🏆 Featured Projects

### 🧬 Scaling, PEFT & KV-Cache Inference in Tiny Decoder-Only Language Models
- **Tech Stack:** Python, PyTorch, Custom BPE Tokenizer, LoRA, KV Caching
- Trained three decoder-only Transformers (992K–1.73M parameters) from scratch on TinyStories, with a byte-pair-encoding tokenizer built from scratch
- Compared LoRA (ranks 4/8/16) against full fine-tuning: LoRA cut trainable parameters by up to 26x, but full fine-tuning kept the best perplexity
- Benchmarked KV-cache vs. non-cached generation latency across model sizes, documented as an independent research manuscript

### 🐟 Self-Supervised Health & Anomaly Detection for Fish in Aquaculture *(Final Year Thesis, in progress)*
- **Tech Stack:** Python, PyTorch, Self-Supervised Learning, Computer Vision
- Developing pretext tasks to learn fish-health representations directly from unlabeled aquaculture video
- Designing anomaly-scoring strategies to flag early disease and stress signs, without relying on large labeled datasets

### 🧠 AI-Based Brain Tumor Detection & Localization
- **Tech Stack:** Python, TensorFlow, CNN, Medical Imaging
- 90% accuracy in tumor identification and localization on MRI scans
- Built a full data pipeline for image preprocessing, augmentation, and bounding-box object detection

### 🤟 Pakistani Sign Language Interpreter
- **Tech Stack:** Python, LSTM, InceptionV3, TensorFlow, Computer Vision
- Dual-stream framework combining transfer learning (static gestures) and an LSTM sequence model (dynamic gestures)
- 98% accuracy across both tasks; submitted as a paper to ICAID 2026 (under review)

### 🏫 University Open House Management Portal
- **Tech Stack:** React, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Node.js
- **Performance:** 40% reduction in query latency, 25% overall system improvement
- Full-stack application with authentication, RBAC, and a RESTful API, used university-wide

### 🎓 TALASH — AI-Powered Academic Recruitment Platform
- **Tech Stack:** Python, FastAPI, Next.js, MongoDB, LangChain, OCR
- Weighted candidate-scoring framework combining academic rankings, publication metrics, and experience
- LangChain-based agentic orchestration for automated evaluation and outreach-email workflows
